## What work this is about

Work information is usually scattered: the contract is in email, the terms are in a chat, the task is in your head,
the invoice template is in an old folder. Here it is gathered in one place and linked together.

## Example

{{example}}
- You ask: “Which contract did we discuss with the packaging supplier?”
  - Answer: Supply contract of 3 March — with a link to the document itself
  - How this is known: The person's page: on 3 March they sent the contract for approval
  - What else is nearby: An open task — return the signed copy

## Three situations

**“Where is that contract?”** You ask which contract was discussed with this person. The answer links to the document
and to the line in the history that records when it was sent.

**“What did they write in March?”** The person's page lists everything you agreed on, by date. There is no need to
reread the correspondence.

**“Make an invoice.”** You say to whom and for how much. The invoice is assembled from your template; if company
details are missing, the system says exactly which ones.

## One day

In the morning you ask for a summary and read one page: who is waiting for an answer and what has changed. During the
day you forward a couple of messages from private chats to the bot — they land in the history of the people they came
from. In the evening you open the day page and see everything that appeared today.
