## What work this is about

A manager does not need to know everything, only what needs their decision today. Here it is gathered on one page from
the same records the work is done in — nothing has to be filled in separately.

## Example

{{example}}
- In the morning you open the dashboard: What needs action?
  - Forgotten deals: Two deals with no movement for more than ten days
  - Overdue payments: A client promised to pay on Friday — no payment
  - Missing documents: One delivery has no sign-off

## Three situations

**“What is urgent?”** The dashboard: forgotten deals, overdue payments, invoices to pay, missing documents.

**“Who has to do what?”** Assignments are recorded with the person and the deadline and are visible both on the shared
page and on the person's page.

**“How much will come in and go out?”** The money page: income and spending by month against the plan, money to
receive and to pay.

## One day

You open the dashboard: it shows what needs action and the questions without an answer. You go down the list, answer
the questions and set two assignments. The rules — how many days count a deal as forgotten, which documents are
required — are set once and changed with one line.
