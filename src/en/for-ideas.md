## What work this is about

A thought is written down, put aside and a month later comes back again — without what was already understood about
it. Here each idea has one page that collects everything said about it.

## Example

{{example}}
- A thought from the phone: “What if the first meeting happened in writing instead of a call?”
  - Idea page: Argument for — it is easier for a person to answer in writing, when it suits them
  - Argument against: In writing you cannot hear where a person hesitates
  - Found earlier: You already wrote about this on 12 September — in other words

## Three situations

**“I have thought about this before.”** A question to your own records finds earlier records on the same topic, even
if the words were different.

**“Why did I decide otherwise back then?”** The idea page has every argument with its date, including those that later
turned out wrong: they are struck through, not deleted.

**“What should I test?”** An idea that is ready to be tested becomes a hypothesis with a deadline and a condition
under which it counts as wrong.

## One day

You write down a thought from the phone. In the evening you discuss it with the system: it finds what you wrote about
it before and adds arguments from both sides to the idea page. What to do next is still up to you.
