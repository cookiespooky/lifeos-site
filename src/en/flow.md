- Email: Emails from people are read in full, newsletters fold into one line. Nothing changes in the mailbox.

## Life OS

Everything that comes in becomes linked records on your computer. From them the system counts, answers and prepares the next step.

- Reads: Every message, email and document is read in full, together with its attachments.
- Matches: Anything new is checked against what is already recorded: the person's history, open tasks, past correspondence, the deal.
- Records: Each fact becomes a separate record with a link to its source: a payment, an invoice, a promise, a deadline.
- Counts: Money, deadlines and stages are counted by rules, the same way every time. What the system does not know, it does not fill in.
- Asks: It does not ask about what is clear. About what is unclear it asks a question and waits for your answer.
- Keeps: Records stay with you as ordinary text files. They open in any editor and remain yours.

## What you get

What it all comes together for: what to do, what to send and how the business is going — at any moment, without manual reconciling.

- Tasks: What to do, with whom and by when — from correspondence and documents, not from memory.
  - Personal
  - For the team
- Documents: Finished files from your templates, with details from your records. What is missing, the system names instead of making it up.
  - Contracts
  - Invoices
  - Sign-offs
  - Quotes
- Summary: The state of the business at any moment: who is waiting for an answer, what is overdue, how much came in and how much you are owed.
- Analytics: Which clients buy more often and for how much, how the business is doing in figures and which posts people read — next to everything else.
  - RFM
  - Business analytics
  - Social media
- Content: Posts for social media — from what is already recorded, published on schedule to the networks you choose.
  - Posts
  - Schedule
