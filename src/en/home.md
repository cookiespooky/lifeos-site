Messages, email, documents, tasks, ideas and money are linked into one system that lives on your own computer.

## Why Life OS {#why}

### Five things that an AI chat and a set of separate apps do not have.

{{benefits}}
- Remembers everything: In an AI chat every conversation starts from zero. Here everything that came in and everything decided is recorded and linked — every day the system knows more about your business.
- Your data stays with you: Records are kept on your computer or on your own server, as ordinary files. Access keys are stored separately from them.
- You decide: The system suggests a decision and shows what it is based on. It does not act on it without your confirmation.
- Low running cost: Repeated work — collecting email, counting money, assembling documents — is programmed and runs without AI. You do not pay for it.
- Fitted to your business: The system is set up for each business separately: your documents, your deal stages, your rules for counting money.

## How it works {#how}

### Everything that comes in and everything you do lands on one map and on the dashboard.

The example is a furniture workshop: two deals, clients, invoices, documents. Under each map, the same steps in words.

#### Message

<div class="lm lm--flow" data-lm="flow"></div>

The system reads a message or an email and matches it with what is already recorded: the history of the person it came
from, open tasks, past correspondence. Then it records what follows from it. The result shows in the summary and on
the dashboard.

1. Message or email — Chats and email: everything that came in.
2. Matching — The person's history, open tasks, past correspondence.
3. Record — A line in the person's history, a new task.
4. Summary and dashboard — Who is waiting for an answer, what changed, what follows from it.

#### Deal

<div class="lm lm--flow" data-lm="deal"></div>

You forward the correspondence and documents of a deal. Each payment, invoice and delivery is recorded separately,
with a link to its source, and the deal page is recalculated from them. What the system does not know, it does not
fill in, but says plainly.

1. Correspondence and documents — Everything that belongs to the deal.
2. Payment, invoice, delivery — Each one as a separate record with a link to its source.
3. Deal page — Revenue, payments, deliveries and the history by date.
4. Dashboard — Forgotten deals, overdue payments, missing documents.

#### Document

<div class="lm lm--flow" data-lm="doc"></div>

You say which document is needed and with whom. The system takes the template and the company details, checks that
every field is filled and assembles the finished file; what is missing, it names instead of making it up.

1. Request — Which document and with whom.
2. Template and details — The template is yours, the details come from the records about organisations.
3. Field check — What is missing is named, not made up.
4. Finished file — A contract, an invoice or an annex, ready to send.

#### Idea

<div class="lm lm--flow" data-lm="idea"></div>

You write down a thought. As it is discussed, arguments for and against are added to it — with the date and whose
they are. When the idea is ready to be tested, it becomes a hypothesis with a deadline and a condition under which it
counts as wrong.

1. Thought — Written as it is, from the computer or the phone.
2. Arguments — For and against, with the date and whose they are.
3. Hypothesis — A deadline and a condition under which it counts as wrong.

#### Question

<div class="lm lm--flow" data-lm="ask"></div>

You can ask about your people, work and records in plain words — and get an answer with links to what it is based on.

1. Question — In plain words, from the computer or the phone.
2. Search — Across everything you have recorded.
3. Answer — With links to the records it comes from.
