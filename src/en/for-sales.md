## What work this is about

When there are many deals and contacts, some of them get forgotten: nobody called back, the invoice was not sent, the
conversation was never picked up again. Here every deal and every person has their own page, and you can see where
nothing has happened for a long time.

## Example

{{example}}
- An email came in: “We will pay the invoice on Friday. Please send us the contract”
  - Person's page: 8 October — promised to pay the invoice on Friday
  - Task: Send the contract
  - Deal: Payment expected on Friday

## Three situations

**“Whom did I promise to call back?”** The promise is recorded as a line in the person's history and as a task with a
deadline.

**“What is going on with this deal?”** The deal page shows the stage, payments, deliveries and the history by date. If
some data is missing, it says so.

**“Which deals have gone quiet?”** A deal with no movement for longer than you set shows up in the list of forgotten
ones — before the client forgets about you.

## One day

A client writes to you in a private chat — you forward it to the bot. A new deal appears in the system at the first
stage, along with a line in the client's history. After a week without movement, this deal will show up in the list of
forgotten ones by itself.
